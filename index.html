The problem

Where does a small business lose leads?

Most small businesses lose leads at the same four points, long before a customer ever says no.

The missed call

You are on a job or with a customer, the phone rings, and the caller hits voicemail. Many of them simply try the next business on the list.

The slow reply

A web form or message sits for hours. By the time you answer, the person has already talked to someone else.

The follow-up that never happens

You send a quote, then get busy. Nobody checks back.

The hard-to-book path

Customers have to call, wait and play phone tag when they would rather pick a time in ten seconds.

None of this is about working harder. Every one of these leaks has a fix. That is what we build.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI marketing agency?

An AI marketing agency uses AI tools to plan, produce and run marketing and customer follow-up faster than a traditional team can, with people checking the work. At Osol AI, that means repairing your lead funnel and running your social media, website and search visibility.

How can AI help my small business get more leads?

AI helps in two ways: it replies to new leads instantly, and it produces marketing consistently. Most small businesses lose leads to slow replies and missed follow-up, so fixing those often helps before you spend more on ads.

What is a lead funnel?

A lead funnel is the path a customer follows from first finding you to booking or buying. A leaky funnel loses people along the way, for example at a missed call, a slow reply, or a missing follow-up.

What is missed call text back?

Missed call text back automatically sends a text to anyone who calls your business and does not get an answer. It tells them you got their call and lets them reply by text, so they do not move on to a competitor.

How fast should I reply to a new lead?

As fast as you can, ideally within minutes. A customer who contacts several businesses often goes with the first one that answers. An automatic first reply means you are never the slow one, even when you are busy.

Can AI run my social media?

AI can draft and schedule posts, but a person should always review them. At Osol AI, we plan your content, produce it with AI, review it for accuracy and brand voice, and share it with you before it goes live.

What are SEO and AEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) helps your business show up in Google. AEO (answer engine optimization) helps it show up in answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. Both depend on clear, accurate content that answers real customer questions.

How much does an AI marketing agency cost?

Price depends on which services you need, how many locations you have, and how much set-up your current systems require. We quote after the free audit, in writing, before any work starts. Our work is month-to-month, so you are not locked in.

How long until I see results?

Faster replies start working the day they go live. Growth from search and social usually takes longer, often several months. We will not promise a specific number of leads, rankings or revenue, because no honest agency can.
